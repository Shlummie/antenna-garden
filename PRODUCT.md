# Antenna Garden

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Delegated by the user's open creative brief: the bundled Sites vinext starter, React, TypeScript, Canvas 2D, and the Web Audio API. The finished artifact must remain useful without external services or runtime network access.

## Users

Inferred from the delegated brief: one curious visitor at a desktop or phone who wants a short, exploratory creative pause rather than a conventional game or productivity task.

## Product Purpose

Antenna Garden is an interactive listening instrument. The visitor tunes through a procedural receiver, steadies unusual signals, keeps fragments, and grows a botanical circuit diagram shaped by how they listened. A successful session produces an authored-feeling garden and a small listening receipt in roughly five to fifteen minutes.

## Positioning

The interface turns the quality and frequency of each captured transmission into plant anatomy, harmonic layers, and a persistent end-state. Listening behavior is the drawing tool.

## Operating Context

The experience begins silently, asks for a deliberate wake gesture, supports pointer, touch, and keyboard input, and can be revisited on the same device. It should feel complete in one browser viewport, with optional deeper discoveries rather than required instructions.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Sound is opt-in and generated locally with Web Audio.
- The visual system is procedural Canvas 2D plus semantic HTML controls and textual equivalents.
- A session can tune, lock, plant, revisit, export, reset, and finish.
- State is versioned, validated, namespaced, and stored only on the device.
- The artifact must be responsive, keyboard-complete, reduced-motion aware, and resilient when audio or storage is unavailable.
- No accounts, analytics, external data, runtime APIs, factual claims, or deployment are required.

## Brand Commitments

The name is Antenna Garden. Its voice is concise, strange, tender, and never threatening. The receiver is lonely rather than haunted. All transmissions and specimens are explicitly fictional procedural content.

## Evidence on Hand

There are no customer claims, commercial claims, or external source assets. The interaction, copy, procedural specimens, and audio are original material created for this artifact.

## Product Principles

- Reward patient listening, including imperfect listening.
- Let behavior leave a visible, reproducible trace.
- Keep mystery legible: every essential action has a clear affordance and a textual equivalent.
- Begin in silence and remain fully meaningful without sound.
- Prefer one deep, coherent instrument over a page of disconnected features.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Essential operation cannot depend solely on canvas, color, sound, hover, or animation. Native controls, live status, visible focus, keyboard tuning, touch support, reduced motion, scalable text, and a silent visual mode are part of the product.
